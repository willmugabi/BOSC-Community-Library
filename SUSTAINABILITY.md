# Sustainability Strategy for BOSC Community Library

## Financial Sustainability

### Phase 1: Grant Funding (Years 1-2)
- Apply for UNESCO digital inclusion grants
- Seek funding from National Library of Uganda
- Partner with NGO technology programs

### Phase 2: Community Contributions (Years 2-3)
- Implement "Friends of the Library" membership (10,000 UGX/month)
- Accept cryptocurrency and mobile money donations
- Corporate sponsorships for feature development

### Phase 3: Earned Revenue (Year 3+)
- Training workshops on digital literacy (15,000 UGX/person)
- Premium support for institutions
- Customization services for other libraries

## Technical Sustainability

### Maintenance Plan
- Monthly security updates
- Quarterly feature releases
- Community bug bounty program

### Volunteer Structure
- 3 core maintainers (technical)
- 5 content moderators
- 2 Luganda translators

## Community Sustainability

### Training Program
- Train 50 librarians annually
- Create video tutorials in local languages
- Establish help desk at partner libraries

## Metrics for Success
- 100 active users by month 6
- 500 resources indexed by year 1
- 5 partner libraries by year 2
